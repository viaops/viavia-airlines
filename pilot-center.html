Operations Information

Current Viavia trip and flight-planning rules.

Automated trip assignment

Available Viavia trips are awarded automatically when successfully claimed. Once awarded, the assignment appears in My Trips, Crew Calendar, and your Crew Portal.

Gate assignments

Viavia gates are not assigned until at least 12 hours before scheduled aircraft departure. Before that window, Flight Planning displays TBD — Gate assignment pending.

Aircraft assignments

Aircraft registrations are released within 24 hours of scheduled departure. Before release, Flight Planning displays the scheduled aircraft variant with the registration pending.
