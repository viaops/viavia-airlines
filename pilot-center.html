Pilot Operations

Automated trip assignment: Available Viavia trips are awarded automatically when successfully claimed. Once awarded, the assignment appears in My Trips and your Pilot Center.

Gate assignment rule: Viavia gates are not assigned until at least 12 hours before scheduled aircraft departure. Before that window, Flight Planning will display: TBD — Gate assignment pending .

Once inside the 12-hour window, Flight Planning can display the assigned gate from Viavia's approved airport gate pool.
