Flight Planning

Flight, pairing, crew and gate assignment information for Viavia Airlines operations.

Gate Assignment Policy Gates are not assigned until at least 12 hours before the aircraft's scheduled departure. Before that window, the gate remains TBD — Gate assignment pending. Once the flight enters the 12-hour assignment window, the system may assign an available gate from Viavia's approved airport gate database.
